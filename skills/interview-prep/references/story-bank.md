# Building a story bank

## Prompts that surface stories

Ask one at a time and let the user talk:

- A time you fixed something nobody asked you to fix.
- A time you disagreed with a manager or colleague. What happened?
- Your proudest result in the last two years. What number moved?
- A time something went wrong and it was partly your fault.
- A time you had too much to do. What did you drop?
- A time you persuaded someone without authority over them.
- A time you learnt something fast to deliver.

## STAR shape (about 2 minutes spoken)

| Part | Share | Tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Situation | 10% | One line of context. |
| Task | 10% | What you were responsible for. |
| Action | 60% | Specific steps, "I" not "we", why you chose them. |
| Result | 20% | Number or honest proxy, plus one line of reflection. |

## Mapping

Build a grid with stories as rows and competencies as columns. Each
competency needs at least two stories so the user is not stuck if one is used
up. Aim for variety: not every story from the same job.

## Probes to rehearse

- "What did you personally do?"
- "What would you do differently?"
- "How did you measure that?"
- "What did the other person say?"

If a story cannot survive two probes, it needs more detail from the user, not
more polish.
